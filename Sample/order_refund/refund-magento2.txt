EXAMPLE ORDER:
========================
Order items:
$10.00 x 1
$2.00 x 1
Total: $12.00,
Shipping: $10.00.
Total Tax applied: $1.95 @8.85%
TOTAL PAID: $23.95
---------------------------------
Case A - shipping refund only: Shipping refund: $1.00

Request:
<refund module="refund">
    <order_increment_id>000000015</order_increment_id>
    <comment_text><![CDATA[refund 1.0]]></comment_text>
    <comment_customer_notify>1</comment_customer_notify>
    <send_email>0</send_email>
    <shipping_amount>1.00</shipping_amount>
    <adjustment_refund>0.00</adjustment_refund>
</refund>

Result: $1.00 + $0.09(proportionate tax) = $1.09 was refunded.

--------------------------------
Case B: adj refund on the above order:
Adj. refund: $2.00

Request:
<refund module="refund">
    <order_increment_id>000000015</order_increment_id>
    <comment_text><![CDATA[refund - adj 2.00 only]]></comment_text>
    <comment_customer_notify>1</comment_customer_notify>
    <send_email>0</send_email>
    <shipping_amount>0.00</shipping_amount>
    <adjustment_refund>2.00</adjustment_refund>
</refund>
Result: $2.00 was refunded.
-------------------------------

Case C: Return all items without shipping on the above order:
<refund module="refund">
    <order_increment_id>000000015</order_increment_id>
    <orderitems>
        <orderitem sku="test003" qty="1" back_to_stock="1"></orderitem>
        <orderitem sku="stepcraft_M1000" qty="1" back_to_stock="1"></orderitem>
    </orderitems>
    <comment_text><![CDATA[sample item refund]]></comment_text>
    <comment_customer_notify>1</comment_customer_notify>
    <send_email>1</send_email>
    <shipping_amount>0.00</shipping_amount>
    <adjustment_refund>0.0</adjustment_refund>
</refund>
Result: $12.00 + $1.06(proportionate tax) = $13.06 was refunded.
=============================

Case D: Money available to refund: ($1.09 + $2.0 + $13.06) = $7.80
Because while refunding shipping and items, tax was already refunded alongwith. If we try to refund due shipping of $9.00 or $7.00, magento will try to include prop. tax, too. So, amount requested will be much more than it took.
To close this order, we can refund via adjustment only.
